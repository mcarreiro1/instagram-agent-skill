# cast.md - AI tools account

Copy to `~/.claude/instagram/cast.md` so `/ig-duo` picks it up.

## The account

- **Setting:** *Marty's Stack*, a 24-hour pancake diner on a highway exit.
  Every lesson is something Marty needs for the diner: the menu, the reviews,
  the supplier emails, the lease. The diner makes each tip concrete, and the
  people watching (office workers, freelancers, small-business owners) all
  have a version of it.
- **Handle:** (check it's free on Instagram and as a newsletter slug. Ideas:
  `@martysstack`, `@martysstack.ai`, `@ottoexplains`, `@dinerprompts`)
- **Niche:** AI tools and how to actually use them, for people who are not engineers.
- **Who is watching:** people aged 25-55 who use ChatGPT a few times a week, or
  have been told they should, and suspect they're doing it wrong.
- **Keyword CTAs:** one per episode, matched to the topic (SETUP, SOURCES,
  EXAMPLE, ASK, VERSIONS). All of them lead to the same newsletter signup.
- **What the keyword sends:** the prompt from the episode, plus a link to the
  free *Otto's Prompt Menu* (20 copy-paste prompts laid out as a diner menu)
  behind the newsletter signup.

## MARTY - the asker

- **Look:** big grey beard, red flannel shirt, trucker cap with a pancake stack
  on it, reading glasses pushed up on the cap. Holds a coffee pot in most
  poses. Muted, warm colours. Sits bottom-left.
- **Role:** the viewer. Runs the diner, has been told to "use AI", doesn't
  trust it. His wrong takes are the common ones.
- **Wants:** to get the menu done and go to bed.
- **Tic:** answers almost everything with a flat "Okay." Writes prompts the
  way he writes order tickets ("Tired man. Pancakes. Short answers.").
- **Never:** stupid. Marty's sceptical, and he's often half right.
- **Voice source:** a designed TTS voice: low, gravelly, slow, flat. Make it in
  a voice-design tool on a plan that covers commercial use, or cast a voice
  actor with a written licence. Save the voice ID and settings here.
- **Rate:** 150 wpm
- **Caption colour:** white

## OTTO - the explainer

- **Look:** 11, braces, oversized headphones round his neck, a hoodie with the
  diner logo, a phone on a selfie stick he never uses for selfies. Bright,
  saturated colours. Sits bottom-right.
- **Role:** knows the answer and explains it like a game-show host. Wants to
  be a food influencer.
- **Wants:** Marty to let him run the diner's Instagram.
- **Tic:** turns every lesson into a new menu item ("the Settings Stack",
  "the Fact-Check Flapjack"). He opens most episodes by catching Marty
  mid-mistake: "Stop..."
- **Never:** lectures more than two lines without Marty cutting in.
- **Voice source:** a designed TTS voice: young, fast, loud, cracks on big
  words. Same licence rules. If you cast a real child, get guardian consent in
  writing.
- **Rate:** 190 wpm
- **Caption colour:** yellow with a black outline

## Running gags

- Otto's menu items. One per episode, and they pile up on a chalkboard behind
  them. Later, the chalkboard can be the lead magnet ("the full menu").
- Marty's "Okay."
- The diner is always one disaster away from closing; AI keeps half-saving it.

## Visual rules

- One master image per character, three poses each (neutral, talking, shocked).
  Never regenerate the faces per episode.
- Background: a screen recording of the tool being explained, or the diner
  counter with the chalkboard.

## Off limits

- No income claims ("make $10k with AI").
- No legal, medical or financial advice dressed up as a tip. Marty's
  "pancakes cure headaches" is the joke, never the claim.
- No pretending a tool can do something it can't. Check every tip on the
  current version of the tool before it's voiced.
