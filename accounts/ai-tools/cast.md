# cast.md - AI tools account

Copy to `~/.claude/instagram/cast.md` so `/ig-duo` picks it up.

## The account

- **Handle:** (pick one that's free on Instagram and as a .com or newsletter
  slug. Ideas: `@novaanddex`, `@askdex.ai`, `@dexplains`, `@promptfight`)
- **Niche:** AI tools and how to actually use them, for people who are not engineers.
- **Who is watching:** people aged 20-45 with office, freelance or small-business
  jobs, who use ChatGPT a few times a week and think they're using it wrong.
  (They're right.)
- **Keyword CTAs:** one per episode, matched to the topic (SETUP, SOURCES,
  EXAMPLE, ASK, VERSIONS). All of them lead to the same newsletter signup.
- **What the keyword sends:** the prompt from the episode, plus a link to the
  free *Dex's Prompt Pack* (20 copy-paste prompts) behind the newsletter signup.

## NOVA - the asker

- **Look:** early 20s, oversized hoodie, phone always in hand, hair in a messy
  bun with a pencil through it. Bright, warm colours. Sits bottom-left.
- **Role:** asks what the viewer is thinking. Confident, wrong, curious.
- **Wants:** to get rich off AI by Friday.
- **Tic:** turns every lesson into a side hustle and announces its number
  ("Side hustle 48"). The number goes up every episode. It's the running gag
  that turns viewers into followers.
- **Never:** dumb for no reason. Nova's wrong guesses are the common ones, so
  viewers recognise themselves and send the Reel to the friend who does it too.
- **Voice source:** a designed TTS voice: young, bright, quick, slightly raspy.
  Make it in a voice-design tool on a plan that covers commercial use, or cast a
  voice actor with a written licence. Save the voice ID and settings here.
- **Rate:** 180 wpm
- **Caption colour:** yellow

## DEX - the explainer

- **Look:** 30s, glasses, one grey streak, a mug with a different slogan every
  episode ("I READ THE DOCS", "CONTEXT WINDOW: FULL"). Cool colours. Sits
  bottom-right.
- **Role:** knows the answer, gives it in one sentence, then regrets it.
- **Wants:** Nova to read the documentation, once.
- **Tic:** opens by stopping Nova mid-mistake ("Stop..."); dry one-liners.
- **Never:** lectures for more than two lines without Nova cutting in.
- **Voice source:** a designed TTS voice: low, dry, unhurried. Same licence rules.
- **Rate:** 160 wpm
- **Caption colour:** white with a cyan outline

## Running gags

- Nova's side-hustle counter. Week 1 runs 48-53; keep counting from there.
- Dex's mug slogan, a different one every episode, never mentioned out loud.
- Nova calls ChatGPT "a genius"; Dex never agrees.

## Off limits

- No income claims ("make $10k with AI"). Nova's side hustles never work, and
  that's the joke.
- No legal, medical or financial advice dressed up as a tip.
- No pretending a tool can do something it can't. Check every tip on the
  current version of the tool before you voice it.
