## 🧾 Marty's ticket

> "I've been told to 'use AI for the business.' I've used it. It writes like a
> brochure, it forgot we close Mondays, and it gave me a study that doesn't
> exist. Why would I keep using it?"
> **- Marty**

Fair question. Here's the honest answer. Most of what goes wrong with
ChatGPT comes down to three habits, and all three are fixable in an
afternoon:

1. **You're not telling it who you are, so it guesses.**
2. **You're trusting the first answer it gives you.**
3. **You're using one chat for everything, forever.**

This week's seven episodes were all about those three. Here's the short
version.

## 🍳 Otto explains: the three fixes

**1. Tell it once, in settings.**
Custom instructions (in ChatGPT: Settings, then Personalization) are a box
where you write who you are, who you work for and how you like answers. It
reads them before every new chat. Prompt #1 on your Prompt Menu is a
fill-in-the-blanks version.

**2. Don't take the first answer.**
Three ways to push back, from the week's episodes:
- **Make it interview you.** End big requests with *"Ask me 3 questions
  before you answer."* It stops guessing and asks about the things it was
  going to guess.
- **Ask for 3 versions.** Short, warm, formal. Pick the parts you like and
  tell it to merge them.
- **Show, don't describe.** "Friendly but professional" means something
  different to everyone. Paste one thing you wrote and say *"match this."*

**3. Start fresh more often.**
Every model can only hold so much text at once. In a very long chat, your
early instructions fade, which is why it "forgets" things. When a chat
passes a few dozen messages, ask for a summary, open a new chat and paste the
summary in. (Prompt #4, the Clean Slate.)

## ⚠️ The one to take seriously

Two episodes this week weren't about getting better answers. They were about
not getting burned.

- **Sources can be invented.** ChatGPT can produce a study, a quote or a link
  that looks real and isn't. Use web search, ask for a link next to every
  claim, and click them. If it won't open, it doesn't go in your work.
- **Check before you paste.** On personal plans, your chats can be used to
  train models unless you switch that off in your data settings. Either way,
  take out names, account numbers and anything you wouldn't tape to the
  register. If your job has an AI policy, follow that first.
