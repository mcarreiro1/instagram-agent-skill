# cast.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/instagram/cast.md` and fill it in. `/ig-duo` reads it
before every script. The cast is the brand on a character account, so build
characters you own: original names, original designs, voices you have the
rights to.

The example below is a working cast for an AI-and-tech explainer account.
Replace it, or keep the shape and change the people.

---

## Character 1 - the ASKER

- **Name:** NOVA
- **Look:** (one sentence an illustrator or image model can draw consistently.
  Save one master image and reuse it; never regenerate the face per episode.)
- **Role:** asks what the viewer is thinking. Confident, wrong, curious.
- **Wants:** to get rich off AI by Friday.
- **Tic:** turns every answer into a get-rich-quick scheme.
- **Never:** is stupid for no reason. NOVA's wrong guesses are the common ones.
- **Voice source:** (a designed TTS voice you created, a stock voice whose
  licence covers commercial use, or a voice actor with a written licence)
- **Rate:** 180 wpm

## Character 2 - the EXPLAINER

- **Name:** DEX
- **Look:**
- **Role:** knows the answer, explains it in one sentence, then regrets it.
- **Wants:** NOVA to read the documentation, once.
- **Tic:** dry one-liners; starts corrections with "No. Well, sort of."
- **Never:** lectures for more than two lines without NOVA cutting in.
- **Voice source:**
- **Rate:** 160 wpm

## Optional character 3 - the WILDCARD

Appears once a week at most. A rival account, a boss, a sentient chatbot.
Leave empty until the first two are working.
